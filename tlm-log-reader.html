TimeSensorValuesRaw (hex)

Layout: 36-byte header blocks and 20-byte data blocks, timestamps in 10 ms ticks, shown relative to the start of each recording. Decoded from NX8+ logs: receiver, signal, ESC, smart battery, Vario-S, gyro, G-force, text display and the transmitter's own sticks and switches. Stick order (throttle, aileron, elevator, rudder) and the knob input are inferred; throttle was checked against ESC throttle. Switch bits, ID 05 and a few smart-battery pages have no known meaning yet and are shown as raw bytes. Cell voltages are always shown per cell. Without a smart plug on the battery they are all identical; with one they differ. Poles and gear ratio only affect RPM.

Drag across the graph to zoom into a time range. The mouse wheel zooms around the pointer, Shift+drag moves the view and double-click shows everything again. Hover to read exact values. Pick a second sensor to draw it over the first one with its own scale on the right; leave it on None to show one graph.
